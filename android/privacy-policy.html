Privacy Policy — Clinical Algorithm Atlas

Effective date: October 27, 2026  ·  Applies to the Clinical Algorithm Atlas Android app

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Clinical Algorithm Atlas Android app, published by Dr. Özgür Karcıoğlu and Dr. Satuk Buğra Han Bozatlı. It applies specifically to this app, not to the authors' other projects or websites.

What the app does

The app is a viewer for the Clinical Algorithm Atlas — a set of interactive, referenced clinical-algorithm web tools the authors publish at satbuhan.github.io/clinical-algorithm-atlas. The app displays that same publicly hosted content inside the app.

Data we collect

We — the app's developers — do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data from your device. The app has no account system, no login, and no analytics or advertising software built into it.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet access — required to load the flowchart content, which is served from GitHub Pages.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or any other device data.

Third-party content

The content shown in the app is served by GitHub Pages (a service of GitHub, Inc.). Loading any web page involves standard server-side technical logs (such as IP address) handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy — we do not have access to that data. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Optional AI chat feature (when available)

This section describes a feature that may be added in a future update and is not necessarily active in every version of the app. It is included here in advance so the policy stays accurate as the feature ships.

If enabled, the app will let you optionally connect your own account with a third-party AI provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) by entering your own API key, to ask questions about the flowcharts or general medical topics. In that case:

  • Your API key is stored only locally on your device, in the device's encrypted storage — we never see it, receive it, or store a copy of it ourselves.
  • Your messages are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you chose, using your own key. We do not operate a server that receives, logs, or stores this traffic.
  • Your use of that provider's AI service is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms, which you agree to separately by using your own account with them.
  • You can remove your stored API key at any time from within the app, which stops the feature from being usable until you enter a key again.

Medical disclaimer

The app is an educational and academic reference tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and it is not a substitute for professional medical judgment. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any patient information or protected health information — it is not designed to accept it in the first place.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and students, not for children, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when the optional AI chat feature above is actually released — we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.
