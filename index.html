Clinical Algorithm Atlas

Interactive, referenced clinical algorithms — one flowchart at a time.

Each tool below keeps an authored flowchart exactly as drawn — every box, decision point, and dose unchanged — and attaches what the step means, why the protocol calls for it, and the sources behind it.

An ongoing collaboration between Dr. Özgür Karcıoğlu and Dr. Satuk Buğra Han Bozatlı.

How these are built

Every algorithm stays byte-for-byte as the authors drew it — boxes, decision diamonds, drug names, and doses are never paraphrased or "corrected" without the author team's sign-off. Only explanatory content is added: what a step means, why it's there, and a citation for every claim, each independently checked against a primary source rather than taken on trust.

Full build methodology (for anyone — person or AI assistant — adding the next tool): METHODOLOGY.md.
